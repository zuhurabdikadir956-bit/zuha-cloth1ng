Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

How can I ask about an order?

Contact us with your order reference and the email address used when placing the order.

When will my order arrive?

Delivery estimates depend on your location and selected option. Check the estimate shown at checkout or in your order updates.

How do I choose the right size?

Review the size guide and fit details on the product page. Contact us if you need help with a particular style.

How do I request a return or exchange?

Check the information provided with your order, then contact our team with your order reference and the item you’re asking about.

Which payment methods can I use?

Available payment methods will be listed at checkout. Review those options before submitting your order.

Can I get help styling an outfit?

Yes. Send us the item you’re considering, the occasion and your question through our contact page.

Where can I find delivery charges?

Any applicable delivery charges and options should appear during checkout before you confirm your order.

What should I include when contacting support?

Include your name, a way to reply, your order reference if relevant, and a clear description of how we can help. Never include passwords or full payment card details.
